# Hex driver (M3 socket head)

Screwdriver with a printed hex tip for M3 socket head screws (ISO 4762: 2.5 mm hex = 2.89 mm corner to corner).
Three tip sizes, because the printer rounds the hex corners: try the biggest that goes in without play.

| Part | Tip corner to corner | Across flats |
|---|---|---|
| `hex-driver-30` | 3.0 mm | 2.60 mm |
| `hex-driver-29` | 2.9 mm | 2.51 mm |
| `hex-driver-28` | 2.8 mm | 2.42 mm |

Size engraved in the bottom. Handle Ø20 × 55 mm, 6 grip grooves, 10 mm tip on a 45° cone.

## Print

`plates/0-pla.stl`: PLA · 0.12 mm · 4 walls · 100 % · no supports. Standing, tip up.

⚠️ A printed 2.5 mm hex is weak: twisting shears it between layers at roughly 0.05–0.1 Nm. Fine for running screws
into printed parts and snugging them by feel; tightening into metal or nuts will twist the tip off. A steel 2.5 mm
hex key is the real fix.
