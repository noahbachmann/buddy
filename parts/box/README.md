# Box

Open-bottom box, 50 × 70 × 30 mm outside, 4 mm side walls, 4 mm top. Inside 42 × 62 × 26 mm. Dimensions in
`params.scad`.

## Print

`plates/0-pla.stl`: PLA · 0.2 mm · no supports. Upside down: closed top on the bed, open side up, so nothing
overhangs (printed the right way up, the top would be a 62 × 42 mm bridge).
